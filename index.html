The Charter

Write a commons that governs itself.

Eight principles, worked out by hand, then signed where no one can quietly rewrite them.

Recorded.

A recorded charter is public and cannot be quietly rewritten — that is the point. It is signed by a key you hold (the signature is the adopting body's seal) and broadcast to several independent relays, so no platform owns the official copy. Drafting here keeps nothing on a server; only you decide when to record it. Built on Elinor Ostrom's eight design principles for enduring commons.
